DRAGON BAY — BRAND ASSET KIT

Source: supplied Visual Identity Guidelines, Version 1 (2024), By Anonymous.

Original artwork
The original SVG files in originals/ are unchanged copies of the nine supplied vectors.

Exports
The logos/ folder contains 22 SVG and 22 PNG exports. The five supplied lockups have original-color, white and black variants. The standalone symbol uses the exact two original symbol paths. Profile assets preserve their supplied backgrounds. Transparent PNGs are 2400px on the longest edge; profile PNGs are 2048 × 2048px.

Guidelines
The PDF retains all 33 pages, live text and vectors, with optimized embedded photographs for a smaller download. Follow the master PDF for clear-space and co-branding construction. Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, outline, add effects to, or rearrange the artwork.

Typography
Helvetica Neue is the brand typeface. Bridger Nature is identified as the basis of the custom wordmark. Fonts are not included; use the outlined logo vectors and obtain appropriate font licenses for typography. The website falls back to Helvetica/Arial when Helvetica Neue is unavailable.
